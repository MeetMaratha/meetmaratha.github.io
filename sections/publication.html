Dynamic routing for efficient waste collection in resource constrained societies
Nature Scientific Reports ↗
Waste collection in developing nations faces multi-fold challenges around resource constraints and real-time changes in waste values. This paper models smart bin and collection vehicle behaviour in a multi-agent environment, formulating a linear program to maximize waste collected while minimizing travel distance. Applied to the city of Chandigarh, India, the approach achieved significantly better performance in waste collected, distance travelled, and bins covered compared to the existing scenario.
